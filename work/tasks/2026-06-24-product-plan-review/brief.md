# WorkScheduleAI Product Plan Review Brief

## Goal

Review `docs/superpowers/specs/work-schedule-ai-product-plan.md` from the perspectives requested by the user:

- frontend developer
- backend developer
- full-stack developer
- development team lead
- AI team lead
- AX team leader
- UI/UX web design team lead
- HR team lead as a likely buyer/user

## Non-goals

- Do not edit the original planning document.
- Do not validate external references or current vendor pricing.
- Do not produce implementation code.

## Success Criteria

- Identify cross-functional planning risks.
- Provide role-specific feedback.
- Call out contradictions or scope ambiguity with concrete document locations.
- Leave a concise verification record.
